💬 Good to know

Questions parents ask

Everything should feel simple, clear and easy to fit into family life.

ASK
AWAY! ✨
What age group is this designed for?+

WonderNest is designed for children aged 2–6, with activities selected to suit different stages of early learning.

How much time does each activity take?+

The core idea is around 20 meaningful minutes a day, with individual activities varying in length.

Is it screen-free?+

Yes. WonderNest activities are designed to encourage hands-on, screen-free learning and parent-child interaction.

What comes in the activity box?+

Activity cards, puzzles, phonics and number games, craft ideas, a reward chart, a 30-day calendar and a parent guide.

Do I need additional materials?+

Most activities are designed around simple things you already have at home. Any special requirements will be clearly explained.

Can siblings use the activities together?+

Absolutely. Many activities can become fun family or sibling challenges, with small adjustments for different ages.
